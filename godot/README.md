# Heat Firm (Godot)

Cozy business idle: grow a single greenhouse from a leaky shed to a five-branch empire, switching between chilli, coffee, flowers, potions, and lollies at will.
Fully offline — no servers, no ads. Your hired staff keep the business running while you're away.

## Requirements

- Godot 4.7.2 (local copy at `..\tools\godot\godot.exe`, i.e. `C:\Users\code\Documents\Default Project\tools\godot\godot.exe`)
- Windows, desktop

## Quick start

Run `tools\run_game.bat`, or manually:

```
& "C:\Users\code\Documents\Default Project\tools\godot\godot.exe" --path "C:\Users\code\Documents\Default Project\heat-firm-godot"
```

## How to play

- Click a plot and plant a seed; the crop grows through 5 visible stages.
- Water a growing plot for a one-time +10% progress boost.
- Harvest when ready. Each harvest rolls a quality — normal / premium / legendary pay a 1x / 2.5x / 7x harvest bonus.
- Craft products from raw stock, then sell on the market or fill customer orders (orders expire).
- Hire staff to automate harvesting, crafting, and selling.
- Buy upgrades and expand the venue (4 levels; each level: +10% sale prices and cheaper plot unlocks).
- Complete the 6 missions per theme and rank up through 8 career ranks.
- Switch between the 5 businesses — each has its own economy, plots, staff, missions, and ranks.

A day lasts 120 seconds; wages are paid at day's end.

## Controls

- Mouse / touch for everything
- `1`–`6` select plot
- `W` water selected plot
- `E` harvest selected plot
- `Q` open Craft tab
- `T` open Theme tab

## Save & offline progression

- Save file: `user://save.json` (Windows: `%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Heat Firm\save.json`)
- Autosave every 5 seconds and on exit
- Offline: on launch, if you were away more than 60 seconds, staff keep working (max 7200s, at 0.8x rate) and a "Welcome back" report appears

## Settings

Menu tab in game: reduced motion, particle level (0/1/2), save now, reset (typed confirm `RESET`).
